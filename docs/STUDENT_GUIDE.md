# Student Guide

Welcome to **Harshwardhan Institute of Ayurveda**. One account works on both the **website** and the **mobile app** — your purchases, progress and test scores stay in sync.

## Create your account
1. Website: click **Start learning**. App: tap **New here? Create an account**.
2. Enter your full name, mobile number, email and a password (at least 6 characters).
3. If asked, open the confirmation email and tap the link, then log in.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the login screen and follow the email link.

## Find a course
- **Courses** shows everything. Filter by your exam (BAMS 1st Prof, AIAPGET, …) or choose **Free**.
- Open a course to see the description, the full curriculum, lesson durations and price.
- Lessons marked **Preview** can be watched for free before buying.

## Buy or enrol
1. On the course / test series / eBook page tap **Buy now** (or **Enrol for free**).
2. Have a coupon? Tap **Have a coupon?** and type the code before paying.
3. Pay with UPI, card, net banking or wallet through Razorpay's secure checkout.
4. You're unlocked immediately. A record appears under **Payments** on your dashboard.

If money was deducted but the course is still locked, wait 5 minutes and refresh. Still locked? Contact us with your **payment ID** (shown by your bank/UPI app and in the Razorpay email).

## Watch lessons
- Open **My learning** (website) or **Me → My courses** (app) and tap a course.
- Tap a lesson to play. Use the speed control (0.75×–2×) and full-screen.
- Download the **Notes PDF** under the video when available.
- Tap **Mark complete** after each lesson — your progress bar fills up so you always know where you stopped.

## Take a test
1. Open **Test series** → your series → **Start test**.
2. Read the instructions: number of questions, time, and negative marking.
3. Tap an option to answer; use the number grid to jump between questions.
4. Tap **Submit** — or the test submits automatically when the timer ends.
5. You'll see your score, correct / wrong / skipped counts and detailed solutions with explanations.

## eBooks & free notes
- **Library → eBooks**: buy once, then tap **Read eBook**.
- **Library → Free notes** (app) or **Free Notes** (website): download any PDF, no purchase needed.

## Live classes
**Live Classes** lists upcoming sessions. A red **LIVE** badge and **Join now** button appear 10 minutes before the class starts. Some classes are only for students of a particular course.

## Updates
New batches, schedule changes and offers appear under the 🔔 bell / **Updates**.

## Rules
Your account is for you only. Sharing your login, recording lectures or forwarding paid PDFs breaks the Terms and may lead to your account being blocked without a refund.

## Need help?
Use **Contact** on the website, or **Chat with us on WhatsApp** in the app (Me tab).
